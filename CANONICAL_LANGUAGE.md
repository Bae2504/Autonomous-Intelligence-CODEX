## Canonical Text

Canonical Text.

validation,

constitutional governance.

No translation

expand,

restrict

established

by

the Canonical Text.

## Interpretive Consistency

Whenever

multiple language editions

the Canonical Text.

Official translations

by

the Canonical Text.

Whenever

is

identified

between

an Official Translation

and

the Canonical Text,

the Canonical Text

shall

prevail.
