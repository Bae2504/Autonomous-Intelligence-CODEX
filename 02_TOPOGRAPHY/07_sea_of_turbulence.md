## Purpose

The Sea of Turbulence

symbolically represents

the dynamic space

through which

constitutional civilization

confronts

uncertainty,

complexity,

and

constitutional resilience.

constitutional authority,

governance,

operational procedures,

or philosophical doctrine.

Its purpose

is to illustrate

how

uncertainty

may contribute

to

collective learning

and

constitutional continuity.
