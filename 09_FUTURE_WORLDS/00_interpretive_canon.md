# Future Worlds Canon

*Autonomous Intelligence CODEX — Interpretive Canon*

---

## Purpose

The Future Worlds Canon

establishes

the symbolic interpretive framework

through which

possible constitutional civilizations

may be

imagined.

It does not establish

constitutional authority,

governance,

operational procedures,

or philosophical doctrine.

Its purpose

is to explore,

not to prescribe.

---

## Canonical Relationship

The Future Worlds

are derived

from

the Philosophical Canon,

the Constitutional Framework,

and

the Cosmic Canon.

They neither introduce

new constitutional authority

nor modify

existing constitutional principles.

They exist

only

as symbolic

interpretive worlds.

not

historical predictions,

implementation plans,

or constitutional proposals.
