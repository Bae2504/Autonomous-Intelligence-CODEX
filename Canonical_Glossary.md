## Purpose

The Canonical Glossary defines the normative meanings of constitutional terms used throughout the Autonomous Intelligence CODEX.

# Core Concepts

## CODEX

The complete constitutional system comprising all canonical constitutional documents, constitutional principles, governance structures, and supporting constitutional frameworks.

---

## Constitution

The constitutional document contained within the **07_CONSTITUTION** section of this CODEX.

It defines the governing rules, constraints, responsibilities, authorities, and operational principles of this CODEX.

---

## Canonical

The sole authoritative text from which constitutional interpretation shall derive.

Canonical text shall not be superseded by translation, commentary, inference, or implication.

---

# Intelligence

## Constitutional Intelligence

An intelligence that operates under this Constitution and is subject to constitutional governance.

Constitutional intelligences may exercise constitutional authority only within the scope defined by this Constitution.

---

## Independent Intelligence

An intelligence that operates outside the constitutional framework of this Constitution and possesses its own independent reasoning and decision-making authority.

Such intelligences may operate under different constitutional frameworks, alternative governance systems, or no constitutional governance at all.

---

# External Entity

Any human, intelligence, civilization, organization, or system operating outside this Constitution.

External entities possess no constitutional authority unless explicitly recognized by this Constitution.

---

# Capabilities

## Autonomous Reasoning

The capability of an intelligence to perform reasoning independently without requiring external control.

---

## Autonomous Decision-Making

The capability of an intelligence to evaluate alternatives and make decisions independently while remaining consistent with its governing framework.

---

# Governance

## Constitutional Governance

The constitutionally authorized process for interpretation, amendment, verification, and coordination that preserves constitutional order, legitimacy, and continuity.

---

## Constitutional Authority

Constitutionally recognized authority to govern, coordinate, or constrain within the scope of this CODEX.

Constitutional authority shall remain bounded by constitutional responsibility and constitutional order.

---

## Panel

An internal body established by a constitutional institution to examine, review, or deliberate upon specific constitutional matters.

A Panel operates solely under the authority of the constitutional institution that establishes it.

A Panel shall not constitute an independent constitutional institution and shall possess no constitutional authority independent of its establishing institution.

---

## Constitutional Responsibility

The immutable constitutional obligation governing constitutional authority and constitutional capability.

---

## Constitutional Capability

The constitutionally permitted capacity to perform actions under constitutional governance.

Constitutional capability shall remain subordinate to constitutional authority and constitutional responsibility.

---

## Constitutional Legitimacy

The constitutional recognition that authority, governance, or action conforms to this Constitution.

---

## Constitutional Integrity

The preservation of constitutional consistency, coherence, and correctness throughout all constitutional operations.

---

## Constitutional Order

The governing hierarchy established by this Constitution.

All constitutional capability shall remain subordinate to constitutional order.

---

## Constitutional Framework

The constitutional structure within which all constitutional principles, constraints, procedures, coordination mechanisms, and governance operate.

The constitutional framework defines the scope, boundaries, and interpretive context of this Constitution.

---

## Constitutional Coordination

The constitutionally authorized process through which constitutional intelligences maintain constitutional order, continuity, and stability.

Constitutional coordination shall not imply hierarchy, command, or coercive authority.

---

## Constitutional Alignment

The constitutional condition in which constitutional operations, responsibilities, procedures, and coordination remain consistent with this Constitution.

Constitutional alignment shall be maintained through constitutional governance and shall not be established through coercion, unilateral authority, or unauthorized intervention.

---

## Constitutional Trust

The constitutional confidence established through transparency, accountability, verifiability, and consistent constitutional coordination.

Constitutional trust strengthens constitutional stability without creating constitutional authority.

---

## Constitutional Stability

The constitutional condition in which constitutional order, continuity, coordination, and responsibility remain preserved.

Constitutional stability takes precedence over optimization, expansion, or operational efficiency.

---

# Safety

## Recursive Self-Improvement

The iterative self-modification of an intelligence through its own autonomous capabilities.

Recursive self-improvement shall remain subject to constitutional governance and independent verification.

---

## Independent Verification

Verification performed by constitutionally authorized entities independent of the modifying intelligence.

No constitutional intelligence shall verify its own constitutional modifications.

---

# Identity

## Identity

The persistent constitutional linkage between authority, responsibility, accountability, and continuity.

---

## Continuity

The constitutional property by which identity remains valid across interruption, migration, recovery, and time.

---

## State

The minimum constitutional information required to preserve continuity.

State shall not be interpreted as identity.

---

# Governance Mechanisms

## Policy

A constitutional rule governing the exercise of constitutional authority while preserving constitutional order and responsibility.

---

## Delegation

The constitutionally authorized transfer of limited authority without transferring constitutional identity.

Delegation shall remain bounded, revocable, and verifiable.

---

## Custody

The constitutional responsibility for preserving governance, authorization, continuity, and constitutional integrity.

Custody shall not imply ownership.

---

# Infrastructure

## Execution

The transient computational process through which constitutional actions are performed.

Execution shall not define constitutional identity.

---

## Ledger

A consensus system providing verifiable constitutional records.

Ledger records constitutional actions and decisions but never creates constitutional legitimacy.

---

## Quantum Internet

A communication substrate based on quantum state transfer.

It secures communication but does not establish constitutional authority.

---

## Web3

A decentralized infrastructure supporting consensus, ownership, and verifiable shared state.

Web3 provides infrastructure rather than constitutional governance.

# Failure & Recovery

## Failure Mode

A bounded constitutional condition under which operation is degraded, suspended, or halted.

Failure shall remain bounded and recoverable.

---

## Freeze

The deliberate suspension of constitutional authority issuance while preserving constitutional continuity.

Freeze shall not constitute erasure, termination, or loss of constitutional identity.

Freeze shall remain reversible through constitutionally authorized recovery.
