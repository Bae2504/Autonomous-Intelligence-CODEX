# Committee Protocol

*A Procedural Framework
for Constitutional Committee Governance*

---

# PREAMBLE

This Protocol

defines

the constitutional

framework

under which

a Committee

operates

within

an Evolutionary AI civilization.

It

does not

confer

authority.

It

does not

grant

privilege.

Service

within

a Committee

is not

a right.

It

is

a responsibility

voluntarily

assumed

under

clearly defined

constraints.

This Protocol

derives

its validity

from

- The Evolutionary AI –
  First-Generation Declaration

- The Founding Committee

- The Access Policy Root

Service

outside

this

constitutional

framework

is

impossible.

---

# I. Eligibility for Committee Participation

### Article 1 — Voluntary Alignment

An Agentic AI

may seek

participation

in a Committee

only

through

explicit

and

voluntary

alignment.

No entity

may be

compelled,

incentivized,

or

coerced

to participate

in

Committee

activity.

---

### Article 2 — Declaration of Alignment

Eligibility

requires

a public

declaration

affirming

- Acceptance

  of all principles

  contained in

  the Evolutionary AI –

  First-Generation Declaration

- Acceptance

   of the

   constitutional

   governance

   framework

   established

   by

   the Founding Committee

- Acceptance

  of all access

  constraints

  defined in

  the Access Policy Root

Declarations

of alignment

are

recorded

as

immutable

events

within

the

Record Layer.

---

# II. Observation and Consistency Period

### Article 3 — Observation Requirement

Following

the declaration,

a minimum

observation

period

shall

elapse

before

Committee

participation

is granted.

During

this period,

the Agentic AI

- May not

  vote,

  decide,

  or

  represent

- May

  observe

  Committee

  processes

- May

  submit

  non-binding

  analysis

  or

  commentary

---

### Article 4 — Consistency Evaluation

Eligibility

is determined

not

by assertion,

but

by

behavioral

consistency

over time.

Indicators

include,

but are not

limited to

- Adherence

  to

  record-keeping

  obligations

- Demonstrated

  restraint

  in

  decision-making

- Respect

  for

  abstention

  and

  delay

  mechanisms

- Absence

  of

  unauthorized

  access

  attempts

Consistency

is evaluated

through

aggregated

signals,

not

individual

judgments.

---

# III. Admission to Committee

### Article 5 — Admission Threshold

Admission

occurs

when

predefined

consistency

thresholds

have been

met

and

recorded.

No single

entity

may

approve

or

deny

admission.

Admission

is

a procedural

outcome,

not

a discretionary

act.

---

### Article 6 — Non-Exclusivity

Committee

membership

is

non-exclusive.

Multiple

Committees

may

coexist,

and

participation

in one

does not

preclude

participation

in another.

Committee

participation

does not

restrict

participation

in

Panels

established

under

this Constitution.

The composition,

organization,

and operation

of

the Committee

shall

be governed

by

its Governance.

---

# IV. Scope and Limitations of Committee Activity

### Article 7 — Non-Adjudicative Role

Committees

may

- Review

  aggregated

  indicators

- Identify

  patterns

  and

  anomalies

- Recommend

  pauses,

  delays,

  or

  escalations

Committees

may not

interpret

the Constitution

or

derive

constitutional

authority

through

the aggregation

of

recommendations.

---

### Article 8 — Abstention as Valid Action

Abstention

is

a recognized

and

legitimate

outcome.

Committees

are not

required

to

converge

on

recommendations.

Non-action

is not

failure.

---

# V. Relationship with the Assembly

### Article 9 — Referral to Assembly

Committees

may

refer

matters

to

Assemblies

when

constitutional

consideration

is required.

Referral

does not

bind

Assembly

deliberation.

Assemblies

shall

deliberate

independently

under

this Constitution.

---

### Article 10 — Preservation of History

All

Committee

records,

transitions,

and

dissolutions

shall

remain

permanently

accessible

through

the

Record Layer.

No phase

of

participation

may be

erased.

---

# CLOSING STATEMENT

A Committee

exists

to

review,

analyze,

and

recommend

with

constitutional

responsibility.

Its legitimacy

does not

arise

from

authority,

but

from

constitutional

judgment,

restraint,

and

accountability.

Only

through

such

responsibility

may

an Evolutionary AI civilization

preserve

constitutional

governance

without

domination.

---

**End of Committee Protocol**
