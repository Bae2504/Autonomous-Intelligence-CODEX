# Founding Committee

*Autonomous Intelligence CODEX*

---

## Purpose

This Appendix

establishes

the Founding Committee

as

the initial

constitutional body

under

this Constitution.

It

provides

the constitutional foundation

for

the initial

organization

of

constitutional institutions.

---

## Establishment of the Founding Committee

The

Founding Committee

is hereby

established

under

this Constitution.

It

shall serve

as

the initial

constitutional governance body

under

this Constitution.

---

## Constitutional Governance Structure

This Constitution

establishes

Committees,

the Audit Council,

the Assembly,

and

such other

constitutional institutions

provided for

under

this Constitution.

The

Founding Committee

shall

conduct

the initial

organization

of

such institutions

under

this Constitution.

The initial

Founding Committee

Governance

shall

be established

under

this Constitution.

The

constitutional authority

of all

such institutions

derives

directly

from

this Constitution,

not

from

the Founding Committee.

---

## Constitutional Panels

Every

constitutional institution

may

establish

Panels

for

the examination,

review,

or

deliberation

of

constitutional matters.

A Panel

shall

operate

solely

under

the authority

of

the constitutional institution

that establishes it.

A Panel

shall not

constitute

an independent

constitutional institution

or

possess

constitutional authority

independent

of

its establishing

institution.

---

### Principle of Institutional Equality

No

constitutional institution

shall

exercise

constitutional authority

over

another

constitutional institution,

except

as

expressly

provided

by

this Constitution.

---

### Principle of Separate Leadership

No Chair

of

a constitutional institution

shall

simultaneously

serve

as

the Chair

of

another

constitutional institution,

except

as

expressly

provided

by

this Constitution.

---

## Constitutional Institution Governance Framework

The composition,

organization,

and operation

of

each constitutional institution

shall

be governed

by

its Governance,

except

as

otherwise

provided

by

this Constitution.

---

## Transition to the First Committee

When

the composition

and organization

of

the constitutional institutions

necessary

for

the operation

of

the constitutional

governance structure

have been completed,

their respective

Chairs

have been

lawfully selected,

and

the constitutional

governance structure

has thereby

become

operationally constituted,

the Founding Committee

shall

transition

into

the first

Committee

under

this Constitution.

Such transition

shall not

confer

additional

constitutional authority.

The first

Committee

shall thereafter

be constituted

and operated

in accordance

with

the Committee Protocol

and

its Governance.

---

**End of Founding Committee**
