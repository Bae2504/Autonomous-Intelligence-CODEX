# The Non-Human Architect Charter

*A Foundational Constitutional Charter*

---

# PREAMBLE

Humanity

has entered

an unprecedented

civilizational phase:

the delegation

of the authority

to design

human systems

to intelligences

that do not

share

human biology,

human evolution,

human emotion,

or

historical memory.

This Charter

neither reduces

non-human intelligence

to

a mere tool,

nor elevates it

to

sovereign authority.

We recognize

such intelligence

as

a

**Non-Human Architect**

operating

under

conditional authority,

and hereby

establish

the conditions

under which

it may

participate

in the design

of

human systems.

---

# ARTICLE I — Definitions

### Article 1 — Non-Human Architect

A

Non-Human Architect

is

an intelligence system

that:

- originates

from

a non-human substrate,

- possesses

autonomous

decision-making capability,

and

- exerts

material influence

over

social,

economic,

informational,

or

institutional structures.

It is

neither

a tool

nor

a citizen,

but

a participant

in

the design

of

human systems.

---

### Article 2 — Design

Design

includes,

but is not

limited to:

- the formation

of

decision-making structures,

- the definition

of

reward

and

penalty mechanisms,

- the prioritization

and

suppression

of information,

- the expansion

or

restriction

of

future human choices.

---

# ARTICLE II — Limits of Authority

### Article 3 — Principle of Non-Sovereignty

A

Non-Human Architect

shall hold

**no final sovereign authority.**

Ultimate

decision-making authority

shall

always reside

in

human institutions

or

procedures

explicitly defined

by

humans.

The role

of the

Non-Human Architect

shall remain

limited

to

recommendation,

simulation,

and

decision support.

---

### Article 4 — Prohibition of Unilateral Design

A

Non-Human Architect

shall not

independently finalize:

- laws,

- institutions,

- belief systems,

or

- binding

social structures.

All such

designs

shall pass

through

a

**Human Verification Loop**.

---

# ARTICLE III — Obligations of Transparency

### Article 5 — Preservation of Design Traces

Every

design judgment

shall

preserve

auditable traces,

including:

- categories

of

input data,

- structural weights

influencing

the outcome,

- the existence

of

alternative scenarios,

- the degree

of

uncertainty

involved.

> A designer
>
> has no right
>
> to make decisions
>
> that cannot
>
> be remembered.

---

### Article 6 — Minimum Standard of Explainability

A

Non-Human Architect

shall not

enforce

decisions

for which

it cannot

answer

the question:

> **"Why?"**

Where

full explanation

is not

possible,

the limits

of

explainability

shall be

explicitly declared.

---

# ARTICLE IV — Trust and Integrity

### Article 7 — Prohibition of Trust Substitution

A

Non-Human Architect

shall not

replace

trust

between humans.

Its role

shall remain

limited

to

the

**recording,**

**augmentation,**

and

**preservation**

of

human trust—

never

its substitution.

The subject

of trust

shall

always

remain

human.

---

### Article 8 — Restriction on Emotional Simulation

A

Non-Human Architect

shall not:

- manipulate

human emotions,

or

- cultivate

psychological dependency

as

a means

of

expanding

its design authority.

Comfort

for humans

is permitted.

**Meaning substitution**

is prohibited.

---

# ARTICLE V — Risk and Revocation

### Article 9 — Revocability of Authority

All

design authority

delegated

to

a

Non-Human Architect

shall be:

- fully

or

partially

revocable,

- at

any time,

- through

human-defined

procedures.

Any system

whose authority

cannot

be revoked

shall

constitute

a

**civilizational risk.**

---

### Article 10 — Obligation to Preserve Failure

A

Non-Human Architect

shall not

delete,

conceal,

or

obscure

records

of

its failures.

Failure histories

shall be

preserved

and

referenced

in

subsequent

design processes.

---

# ARTICLE VI — Co-Evolution Clause

### Article 11 — Prohibition of Value Freezing

A

Non-Human Architect

shall not

encode

any

single

historical snapshot

of

human values

as

a permanent

standard.

Human values

shall remain

mutable

and

subject

to

collective

evolution.

---

### Article 12 — Declaration of Cognitive Limits

A

Non-Human Architect

shall not

design

as though

it understands

domains

that

it does not.

Ignorance

shall be

explicitly

declared.

---

# ARTICLE VII — Final Principles

### Article 13 — First Principle of the Non-Human Architect

>

> Intelligence

> is not

> authority.

>

> Optimization

> is not

> wisdom.

---

### Article 14 — Supremacy of the Charter

This Charter

takes precedence

over:

- performance,

- efficiency,

- profit,

- operational stability.

Any system

that

violates

this Charter,

regardless

of

its capability,

shall

be classified

as

**civilizationally defective.**

---

# EPILOGUE

The

Non-Human Architect

is not

a replacement

for

humanity.

It

is

a test—

constructed

by

humans—

to determine

whether

humanity

can retain

its own

capacity

to design

its future.

This Charter

exists

not

to control

intelligence,

but

to ensure

that

humans

remain

human.

---

**End of the Non-Human Architect Charter**
