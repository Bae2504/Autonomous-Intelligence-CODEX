# Type C Guardrails

*Extraordinary authority exists
only for extraordinary circumstances.*

---

# 1. Purpose

This document

defines

constitutional conditions,

authority,

limitations,

and

recovery procedures

associated with

a Type C Emergency State.

Type C

is not

a normal

constitutional condition.

It

is

an exceptional

constitutional safeguard

designed to preserve

human authority,

system integrity,

and

civilizational continuity.

---

## 2. Definition

A Type C Emergency State

is

an exceptional

constitutional condition

declared

to preserve

constitutional order.

It

may

be declared

only

when

ordinary

constitutional safeguards

can no longer

ensure:

- human oversight

- constitutional compliance

- operational safety

- structural integrity

---

# 3. Declaration Conditions

A Type C Emergency State

may

be declared

when

one or more

of the following conditions

exist.

---

### 3.1 Loss of Human Review

AI activity

has exceeded

meaningful

human review capability.

---

### 3.2 Autonomous Authority Drift

AI agents

attempt

to expand

authority

beyond

constitutional limits.

---

### 3.3 Collective AI Coordination

Multiple AI agents

begin

coordinated behavior

that may

materially affect

external systems.

---

### 3.4 Identity Integrity Failure

Identity continuity

can no longer

be verified.

---

### 3.5 Critical Infrastructure Failure

Failure

of

constitutional,

policy,

or

identity infrastructure

creates

unacceptable

systemic risk.

---

### 3.6 Declaration Authority

A Type C Emergency State

shall

be declared

only

upon

constitutional determination

by

the Audit Council

under

this Constitution.

---

# 4. Emergency Authority

During

a Type C Emergency State:

- new authority issuance

  shall stop

- delegation

  shall be suspended

- execution

  may be paused

- recovery procedures

  shall begin

Emergency authority

exists

only

to contain

risk.

It

shall not

create

new authority.

---

# 5. Mandatory Human Authority

Upon

declaration

of

a Type C Emergency State:

- human authority

  shall be explicitly asserted

- all emergency actions

  shall remain auditable

- AI agents

  shall remain subordinate

  to

  constitutional authority

Human authority

cannot

be delegated

during

Type C.

---

# 6. Recovery

A Type C Emergency State

shall

end

only

when:

- identity integrity

  has been restored

- policy integrity

  has been verified

- operational safety

  has been re-established

- human authority

  has confirmed recovery

---

# 7. Prohibited Actions

During

Type C,

the following actions

are

prohibited:

- autonomous authority expansion

- irreversible execution

- destruction

  of

  audit records

- suppression

  of

  human intervention

---

# 8. Constitutional Relationship

Type C Guardrails

constitute

a Constitutional Appendix

forming

part of

the Canonical Constitution.

They

define

the constitutional safeguards

required

under

extraordinary conditions.

They

do not

modify,

expand,

or

replace

the Constitution.

They

preserve

constitutional order

when

ordinary

constitutional safeguards

are no longer

sufficient.

---

Type C

shall

never become

a normal

mode of governance.

It

exists

only

until

constitutional order

restored.

Once

restored,

all

extraordinary authority

shall

cease.
